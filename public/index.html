        HOW TO PLAY

        Swipe (or arrow keys / WASD) to steer your Bit through an endless maze.

        The Void rises from below and speeds up. Keep climbing!

        Eat dots back-to-back to build your chain. Every 30 adds to your multiplier. Hit 300 for a NOVA that wipes the screen.

        Grab a Surge Core to make enemies vulnerable, then eat them for big points.

        Equip up to 3 power-ups; they show up in the maze. Collect ◆ coins to unlock and upgrade more.
